# 🍻 Nai Nights – Let Chaos Decide!

Welcome to **Nai Nights**, the ultimate party game app designed to spice up your gatherings with fun and interactive games! Whether you're hosting a small get-together or a full-blown party, Nai Nights has something for everyone. Let chaos decide the fun!

---

## 🎮 Features

### 1. **Drink Generator**
   - 🍹 Generate random drinks and challenges to keep the party lively!
   - Perfect for breaking the ice or keeping the fun going.

### 2. **Who Pays?**
   - 💸 Spin the wheel to decide who pays for the next round!
   - A fun and fair way to settle the bill.

### 3. **Drunk Jenga**
   - 🪵 Play Jenga with a tipsy twist!
   - Each block comes with a challenge or dare. How long can you keep the tower standing?

### 4. **Never Have I Ever**
   - 🙊 Confess your wildest secrets or take a drink!
   - A classic game with a boozy twist.

### 5. **Truth or Drink**
   - 🍻 Answer truthfully or take a drink!
   - Test your honesty and your liver in this hilarious game.
